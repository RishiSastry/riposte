# E021 — unknown name / state field

**Cause:** You accessed a field that isn't part of the state surface (e.g. a typo like
`my.active.hp` instead of `my.active.hp_fraction`, or `opponent.active.speed` instead of
`opponent.active.stats.spe`).

**Fix:** see the `state_surface` topic for the exact field names. Remember: HP is always
`hp_fraction` (quirk Q1), and stats live under `stats.<atk|def|spa|spd|spe>`.
